# Welcome to Your Record Space

---

## 1. Uploading Files
- Click the **“+”** or **“Upload”** button to add files.  
- Or simply **drag and drop** files from your computer into the window.  

---

## 2. Organizing Files
- Create folders with **“New Folder”** to group related files.  
- Move files by dragging them into folders.

---

## 3. Sharing Files
- Right-click a file or folder and select **“Share”**.  
- Copy the link or add people by email.  
- You can set **view or edit** permissions when sharing.

---

## 4. Mounted Files
- If you have a local mount, moving file there will make them appear automatically in your space.
- Mounted files may not move or delete correctly in Nextcloud (use your local mount for that).
- Mounted files can still be shared through Nextcloud like regular uploads.

---

## 5. Review Process
- Under the **review** process, files are **locked** and cannot be edited until the review is complete.  
- You can still view and download them.

---

## 6. Quick Navigation
- Use the **search bar** at the top to quickly find files.  
- Sort by name, size, or modified date using the column headers.  
- Use the **breadcrumb path** (top bar) to jump back to parent folders.

---

## 7. Tips for Success
- Use the [Nextcloud Desktop App](https://nextcloud.com/install/#desktop-files) for large files or lots of files. It can resume uploads and is more reliable than the browser.
- Upload files in batches instead of dragging thousands of files at once.
- Compress folders into ZIPs before uploading many small files.
- Prefer stable, wired connections for very large files (>2 GB) to avoid restarts on interruption.
- Avoid moving huge folders around mid-upload.
